# Old Sarge's Surplus — Official Fort Liberty / Spring Lake Digital Storefront & Inventory OS

- **Physical Storefront:** 240 S Bragg Blvd, Spring Lake, NC 28390 · `(910) 497-5335`
- **Official Domain:** `https://oldsargessurplus.com`
- **Verified eBay Storefront:** [`oldsargessurplus`](https://www.ebay.com/usr/oldsargessurplus)

## Core Capabilities

1. **Entity Disambiguation & Reputation Shield:** Stops mistaken-identity Google Reviews intended for the unrelated New York website (*Old Sarge's Drop Zone*, Westfield, NY) via Schema.org `disambiguatingDescription`, `llms.txt`, and an interactive consumer verification modal.
2. **8-Bay Live Surplus & NSN Inventory Matrix:** Searchable USGI surplus catalog by keyword, National Stock Number (`NSN`), and CIF Turn-In essentials across 3 switchable visual themes (`Hangar Gold & Steel`, `Fort Liberty Olive Drab`, `Daylight Counter`).
3. **Interactive Fort Liberty CIF Turn-In Savings Calculator & 24-Hr Counter Hold Sheet:** Real-time comparison between Fort Liberty CIF Statement-of-Charges costs and Old Sarge's Surplus prices, with 1-tap SMS and telephone hold requests.
4. **10-Second Owner Admin & On-Site Sewing Switchboard:** Instant 1-tap stock toggles and sewing turnaround status updates (`Walk-In Same-Day`, `24-Hr Drop-Off`, `Heavy Ruck & Zipper Repair`).
5. **1080p Counter TV Digital Signage & Offline USB Export:** High-contrast 16:9 display board with 1-click `.PNG` export for offline USB TV playback.
