## 1. Executive Summary: The Mistaken-Identity Review Crisis

For over 30 years, **Old Sarge's Surplus** at **240 S Bragg Blvd, Spring Lake, NC** has served Fort Liberty service members, veterans, and outdoor enthusiasts with genuine USGI military surplus and on-site uniform sewing, earning a strong **4.3★ Google Maps rating**.

However, because `oldsargessurplus.com` was sitting behind a locked Shopify `/password` gate (`x-robots-tag: nofollow`), Google's search index and AI summaries merged the Spring Lake storefront with a completely unrelated mail-order website in Westfield, New York called **Old Sarge's Drop Zone (`oldsargesdropzone.com`)**. When online customers of the New York website experienced unshipped orders and initiated bank chargebacks, they searched *"Old Sarge Surplus"* on Google Maps and left 1-star reviews on the **Spring Lake, NC** Google Business Profile.

   - Injects authoritative **Schema.org `Store` + `LocalBusiness` JSON-LD** with an explicit `disambiguatingDescription` and `llms.txt` AI index so Google, Apple Maps, ChatGPT, and Gemini permanently separate the two businesses.
2. **8-Bay Live Surplus & NSN Locker Matrix (Eliminates Shopify SKU Burnout):**
   - Replaces the locked `$39–$105/month` Shopify password screen with a high-speed, zero-monthly-fee inventory platform organized into **8 Tactical Bays** (*Rucks & Packs, Pouches & LBE, Sleep & ECWCS, Uniforms & Jackets, Combat Boots, Ammo Cans & Hardware, Helmets & Survival, On-Site Sewing*).

   - Lets soldiers check off missing packing-list or CIF turn-in items, calculates their instant savings vs. CIF Statement-of-Charges deductions, and generates a **24-Hour Counter Hold Pull-Sheet** they can text or call in to `(910) 497-5335`.
